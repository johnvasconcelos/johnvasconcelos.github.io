4. How normal is the sampling distribution?

Normal Q-Q plot · points on the line = normal; curved ends = skewness or heavy tails

Skewness as n grows · theoretical curve: population skewness / √n

Verdict rule: close to normal if |skewness| < 0.25 and |excess kurtosis| < 0.5; clearly non-normal if |skewness| ≥ 0.75 or |excess kurtosis| ≥ 1.5. With many samples, Shapiro-Wilk rejects even tiny departures, so read the p-value together with skewness, kurtosis and the Q-Q plot.
