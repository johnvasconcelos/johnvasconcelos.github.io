ANOVA Residuals: Why Do We Pool Them?

Three groups, each drawn from a normal population with the same σ. Pooled together, the raw values of y can look nothing like a normal. Subtract each group's mean and every group slides to zero — the three curves land on top of each other and the pooled residuals form a single normal.
