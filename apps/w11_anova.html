One-Way ANOVA: Testing Means with Two Estimates of σ²

Three populations, one sample from each. Drag the distance between the population means. At zero the populations are identical and H0 is true. As they pull apart, the group means spread out more than chance alone would explain: the between-groups estimate of σ² inflates, while the within-groups estimate stays put (it only looks inside each group, so it is the ruler). Their ratio is F.
