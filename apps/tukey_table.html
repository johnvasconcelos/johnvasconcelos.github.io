Tukey q Table (Studentized Range)

Find the critical value q, or the p-value for an observed q, of the studentized range distribution Q(k, df). Here k is the number of group means compared and df is the error degrees of freedom from the ANOVA (N − k). Q is the range of k sample means divided by its estimated standard error, Q = (ȳmax − ȳmin) / √(MSE/n). It is used in Tukey's HSD test after an ANOVA: two means differ when |ȳᵢ − ȳⱼ| ≥ HSD = qα; k, df · √(MSE/n). Only the right tail is used, and the distribution takes only positive values.

Solve for
One-way ANOVA with k groups and N observations: df = N − k. k is a whole number from 2 to 100; df ≥ 2. Type “inf” for df = ∞.
