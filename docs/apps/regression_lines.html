Regression: the True Line vs. the Sample Lines

A population is generated by the model Yi = β0 + β1Xi + εi (here X = years of schooling and Y = salary in $1,000s). The true line E[Y | X] = β0 + β1X passes through the means of Y for each X, not through the points. In practice nobody sees it: we only see a sample, fit OLS, and get an estimated line Ŷ = β̂0 + β̂1X. Draw many samples: every line misses the true one a little, but on average they hit it.
