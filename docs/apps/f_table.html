F Table (F-Table)

Find the area under the F curve for a value and its two degrees of freedom, or the critical value F that gives a chosen area. F ~ F(df₁, df₂), the ratio of two independent chi-square variables, each divided by its df: F = (χ²₁/df₁) / (χ²₂/df₂). It is used to compare two variances (F = s₁²/s₂²) and in ANOVA. The distribution is skewed right and takes only positive values; a left-tail critical value is the reciprocal of a right-tail value with the df swapped.
